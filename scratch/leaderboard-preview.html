Points combine simulations completed and score improvement — not raw averages, so consistent practice counts as much as high scores.
Why this shows rank ± 5, not top 10

Students outside the top ranks still see themselves in context, next to peers close to their level, rather than a wall of unreachable names. This is the detail most likely to affect whether lower-ranked students keep engaging.

Why streak/volume shows next to score

Surfacing "simulations completed" alongside points reinforces that the score rewards practice frequency, not just talent — so the mechanic is visible, not just the outcome.

What the segmented control does

Daily resets create urgency; weekly (shown, default) is the primary engagement driver; monthly rewards sustained study over exam-prep season.
